# Privacy, Ethics and Safety

## Dignity principle
Heat adaptation support must not become a system for extracting more work from people in unsafe conditions.

### Product rule
The planner may suggest lower-exposure choices. It must never phrase the goal as “maximize deliveries despite heat.”

## Anti-surveillance
Prototype does not require:
- continuous GPS;
- detailed delivery history;
- health diagnoses;
- employer performance scores.

Use coarse zone and explicit user constraints.

## Consent and purpose
Future deployments should state:
- what data is collected;
- why it is needed;
- who sees it;
- retention period;
- whether it affects benefits;
- how to appeal/correct data.

## Safety boundary
The app is not medical advice and cannot certify safe work. Emergency symptoms require official/local emergency guidance, not schedule optimization.

## Fairness
Sponsor allocation scenarios can create exclusion. The UI must show uncovered workers and the policy reason rather than hiding them. Sensitive attributes should not be inferred for “vulnerability scoring.”

## Automation boundary
- AI: planning/explanation only.
- deterministic engine: financial eligibility/commitment.
- human: policy publication, disputes/manual review, real-world program governance.

## Misuse cases
Explicitly prohibit using HeatReserve to:
- penalize workers who decline a recommended schedule;
- infer health status;
- pressure workers into working during dangerous warnings;
- optimize employer productivity at the expense of worker choice;
- deny statutory protections.
