# 2–3 Minute Video Script

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem
“Heat alerts already tell outdoor workers when conditions are dangerous. But for a delivery rider paid by the job, following that warning can mean losing the money they need today. A randomized study with 276 gig delivery workers in Delhi and Gurugram found that small, timely heat-triggered cash transfers helped workers shift away from the hottest hours and use cooled breaks compared with warnings alone.”

On screen: source card + one carefully labeled research finding.

## 0:20–0:35 — Product
“HeatReserve turns that insight into an adaptation operating system. A sponsor funds a reserve. When a verified heat episode qualifies under a published policy, the system creates support and then helps the worker make a lower-exposure plan.”

## 0:35–0:58 — Trigger
Show Judge Mode.

“This heat warning is a frozen source snapshot. The policy engine checks the event, worker eligibility and reserve. The demo creates a ₹200 simulated commitment because that matches the amount tested in the cited research. AI is not involved in this financial decision.”

## 0:58–1:23 — AI
“Now AI does the job it is good at. It receives typed facts: hourly conditions, this worker's available hours and verified cooling points. It proposes a plan that shifts work away from the highest-burden window and adds a cooled break. A deterministic verifier rejects invented places, impossible times and claims like ‘safe to work.’ If the model fails, HeatReserve falls back to a deterministic planner.”

## 1:23–1:42 — Receipt
“Every result becomes a Decision Receipt. It records the source snapshots, policy version, reason codes, planner tools and software versions. The canonical receipt is hashed. If I change the amount in this copy, verification fails.”

Perform tamper click.

## 1:42–2:04 — Sponsor allocation trade-offs
“A funder also has to answer a harder question: when the reserve cannot cover everyone, how should support be allocated? HeatReserve compares equal, burden-first and fairness-constrained strategies under the exact same budget, then reports coverage and modeled high-heat exposure shifted per thousand rupees.”

## 2:04–2:25 — Evidence honesty + SDG
“We keep research, measurements and simulation separate. The worker-study result is external research. Our reliability tests are measured. This impact scenario is simulated. HeatReserve is designed around SDG 8.8 on safe and secure working environments, with SDG 13.1 on climate resilience as a secondary target.”

## 2:25–2:38 — Close
“Existing heat programs prove financial protection can scale. HeatReserve adds the missing operational layer between a warning and an action a worker can actually take. Warnings tell workers what to avoid. HeatReserve gives them room to act.”
