# Project Description — Draft

## HeatReserve
**Warnings tell workers what to avoid. HeatReserve gives them room to act.**

Outdoor gig workers can face a brutal trade-off during extreme heat. A warning may tell them to avoid the hottest hours, but if they are paid per delivery, following that advice can mean losing income immediately.

HeatReserve is an anticipatory climate-adaptation platform built around that gap. A sponsor funds an adaptation reserve. When a verified heat episode meets a published policy, HeatReserve deterministically creates an adaptation commitment for an eligible worker. It then uses a grounded AI planner to combine hourly conditions, the worker's schedule constraints and verified cooling locations into a lower-exposure work plan.

The financial decision is never made by AI. Eligibility, amount, reserve accounting and reason codes are handled by a versioned deterministic policy engine with idempotent transactions. AI is limited to planning and explanation, and every plan passes a deterministic verifier that blocks invented facilities, impossible schedules and unsafe claims.

Every consequential result becomes a Decision Receipt containing source snapshot IDs, policy and software versions, reason codes, AI/tool provenance and a tamper-evident SHA-256 digest. A replay-first Judge Mode lets anyone reproduce the demonstration from frozen evidence even when external APIs are offline.

HeatReserve also gives funders a fixed-budget scenario tool that compares equal, burden-first and fairness-constrained allocation strategies. Results are labeled as research, measured, simulated or target so external evidence is never confused with prototype impact.

The idea is grounded in research with gig delivery workers in Delhi and Gurugram showing that timely heat-triggered cash can enable schedule shifts and cooled breaks when warnings alone are not enough. HeatReserve does not claim those research outcomes as its own. Instead, it turns the mechanism into inspectable, scalable decision infrastructure.

Primary SDG alignment: **SDG 8.8**, safe and secure working environments, including precarious workers. Secondary alignment: **SDG 13.1**, climate resilience and adaptive capacity.
