# Judge Q&A Defense Sheet

## “Isn't this just heat insurance?”
Existing heat finance is real and we cite it. HeatReserve is not claiming to invent parametric payouts. Our focus is the operating layer: sponsor reserve policy, individualized grounded adaptation planning, budget/fairness allocation, replay and auditable Decision Receipts. The default model also does not require workers to prepay.

## “Why does this need AI?”
It does not need AI for the core financial mechanism. That is intentional. AI is used where multi-constraint composition and plain-language explanation can help. We benchmark it against a deterministic fallback and reject invalid outputs. If AI adds no measurable value, the fallback still works.

## “Why not just send ₹200 automatically?”
Transfer execution is only one step. A real program needs verified trigger provenance, policy versions, reserve integrity, duplicate prevention, worker-specific feasible adaptation actions, fund allocation when budget is limited, and auditability. HeatReserve makes that whole path inspectable.

## “Is ₹200 enough?”
We do not claim it is a production-optimal amount. ₹200 is a research-inspired replay setting because that was the transfer amount in the cited randomized study. A production amount requires local program economics and evaluation.

## “Are you saying your plan is safe?”
No. HeatReserve never certifies safety. It compares relative modeled heat burden and preserves official warnings/guidance. Occupational heat stress depends on factors beyond weather, so the UI says lower exposure, not safe.

## “How do you know this improves health?”
We do not claim HeatReserve has measured a health effect. We cite external causal research for the underlying liquidity mechanism and report our own engineering measurements and simulations separately.

## “What if the AI hallucinates a cooling center?”
It cannot reach the accepted plan. Cooling locations are typed tool facts with IDs; the deterministic verifier rejects unknown IDs. Judge Mode includes an adversarial test for this.

## “What if the warning arrives twice?”
The commitment uses a deterministic idempotency key with a database uniqueness constraint. Repeat requests return the existing commitment and do not reduce the reserve again.

## “What if the AI provider goes down during judging?”
Judge Mode uses frozen evidence and deterministic fallback / pinned local behavior. Financial decisions never depend on the model.

## “Where does this scale?”
The architecture computes shared event/weather facts per zone and fans out only worker-specific planning. Policy and reserve are tenant-versioned. The same operating model could support other heat-exposed sectors after policy/user validation.

## “Who pays?”
The prototype models sponsor-funded reserves. Potential sponsor categories include platforms, municipalities, CSR/philanthropic climate programs, worker organizations and regulated financial partners. We do not claim any confirmed partnership without evidence.
